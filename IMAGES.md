### 1. In-app background (`img/hero.webp`)
> A tall vertical painting (4:5) of a West Virginia mountain night in early winter. Deep midnight-plum sky full of soft stars, layered blue-black ridgelines fading into mist, a light dusting of snow on the nearest hill, and one small cabin window glowing warm gold far down in a hollow. The center of the image is calm, empty dark sky, because an Advent wreath will be placed over it. Painterly realism, very dark overall, low contrast, no text, no people, no wreath, no candles.

*Save as WebP about 1440px tall. The app dims it to 55%, so dark is good.*

### 2. Launch post (`social/launch.png`)
> Square image (1:1). An Advent wreath of fresh fir, holly and red berries sits on a worn wooden farmhouse table by a frosted window. Through the window: snowy West Virginia ridgelines at blue hour. Wreath candles: three purple, one rose, and a tall white center candle, all unlit, with a single match held just above the first purple candle, about to light it. Warm lamplight, deep plum and evergreen tones, painterly realism, reverent and cozy, no text, no faces.

### 3–7. Weekly Sunday posts
Use the same base prompt for each and change only the bracketed line:

> Square image (1:1). Close-up of an Advent wreath of fresh fir, holly and red berries with pinecones, on a worn wooden farmhouse table. Candles: three purple, one rose, and a tall white center candle. **[LIT LINE]** Background: a frosted window and soft-focus snowy Appalachian ridges at night. Warm candlelight glow on the greenery, deep plum and midnight tones, painterly realism, reverent, cozy, no text, no people.

| Image | LIT LINE |
|---|---|
| week-hope | Only one purple candle is lit; the rest are unlit. Mood: quiet expectation, first light in the dark. |
| week-peace | Two purple candles are lit; the rose and white candles are unlit. Mood: still, calm snowfall outside. |
| week-joy | Two purple candles and the rose candle are lit; one purple and the white candle are unlit. Mood: warmer, a little brighter, rosy glow. |
| week-love | All four outer candles are lit; the white center candle is unlit. Mood: full warm glow, a quilt folded nearby. |
| christmas-eve | All five candles are lit, the white center candle brightest. A small wooden nativity is softly visible behind the wreath. Mood: holy night, golden glow. |

**Tip for consistency:** generate week-hope first, then tell ChatGPT: *"Same wreath, same table, same camera angle — now [LIT LINE]."*
